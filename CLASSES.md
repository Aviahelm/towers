## Knight

- **Body**
    
    - **The Core Idea:** Raw, unyielding physicality. This path governs how hard you hit, how tough your material framework is, and how deep your internal engine of life and stamina runs.
        
    - **Progression Theme:** Starts with enhanced athletic output and dense skin, scales up to shockwave impacts and biological resistance, and peaks at absolute indestructibility and eternal life-force.
        
- **Technique**
    
    - **The Core Idea:** Flawless technique, micro-precision, and absolute physiological dominion. This path governs how efficiently you move, how you bypass obstacles through geometry and timing, and how consciously you steer your own nervous system.
        
    - **Progression Theme:** Starts with autonomic overrides and silent footwork, scales up to targeted healing and momentum preservation, and peaks at absolute anatomical sovereignty and pre-determined outcome enforcement.

## Mage

- **Form 
    
    - **The Core Idea:** The manipulation of physical presence, geometry, and substance. This path governs what things are made of, where they are located, and the volume of space they occupy.
        
    - **Progression Theme:** Starts with basic phase transitions and short-range spatial steps, scales up to material transmutations and pocket dimensions, and peaks at absolute dominion over physical matter and distance.
        
- **Force**
    
    - **Motto/Core Idea:** The manipulation of dynamic power and conceptual axioms. This path governs thermodynamic/kinetic energy fields and the enforcement of absolute rules, constraints, and universal truths.
        
    - **Progression Theme:** Starts with thermal sparks and basic behavioral restrictions, scales up to electromagnetic flows and ontological counter-laws, and peaks at absolute dominion over energy states and reality's fundamental code.


## Monk

- **Flow**
    
    - **The Core Idea:** Complete physical grace, friction-free movement, and the temporary shedding of material weight. This path governs how you navigate space, redirect incoming kinetic forces, and move without hesitation.
        
    - **Progression Theme:** Starts with balanced footwork and frictionless movement, scales up to weightless steps and spatial glissades, and peaks at absolute inertial mastery and freedom from gravity.
        
- **Mind**
    
    - **The Core Idea:** Absolute mental clarity, pre-cognitive awareness, and ironclad willpower. This path governs how you read your environment, anticipate threats before they occur, and project your inner focus outward as an unyielding aura.
        
    - **Progression Theme:** Starts with heightened sensory baselines and anchored composure, scales up to micro-tell reading and predictive tactical calculation, and peaks at intent-reading and absolute real-time awareness.

## Patron

- **Bond**
    
    - **The Core Idea:** The internal relationship, alignment, and merging of consciousness with the entity. This path governs how deeply you tune into the Patron's frequency, adopt their worldview, and share your physical vessel with them.
        
    - **Progression Theme:** Starts with faint emotional echoes and intuitive reframing, scales up to sensory sharing, cognitive translation, and co-pilot reflexes, and peaks at total identity unification and absolute synchronization.
        
- **Authority**
    
    - **The Core Idea:** Externalizing the entity's power, environment, and rules onto the material plane. This path governs how you project the Patron's aura, overwrite local physical laws, and manifest their actual scale through your actions.
        
    - **Progression Theme:** Starts with ambient psychological and environmental bleeds, scales up to localized domain encroachment and borrowed might, and peaks at sovereign extensions and total environmental rewriting.
