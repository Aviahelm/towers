### Knight Archetype: The Physical Paragon
#### 1. Body

- **Circle 1: The Enduring Engine** — Optimized circulation and stamina, allowing hours of heavy physical exertion without breathlessness or premature fatigue.
    
- **Circle 2: The Greater Resilience** — Enhanced metabolic control and recovery, letting you easily withstand freezing cold, blistering heat, or minor blood loss.
    
- **Circle 3: The Exceptional Endurance** — Accelerated toxin purging and cellular repair, causing poisons to wear off in seconds and wounds to close rapidly.
    
- **Circle 4: The Masterful Equilibrium** — Total systemic homeostasis that decouples your biological engines from the immediate need for constant rest, food, or air.
    
- **Circle 5: The Supernatural Vigor** — Mana-infused life support where internal biological systems are sustained directly by mana, enabling mid-combat tissue knitting.
    
- **Circle 6: The Transcendent Engine** — Absolute immunity to biological failure, freeing your form from the concepts of exhaustion, aging, and natural death.
    
- **Circle 7: Absolute Vitality** — The ultimate manifestation of eternal life-force where your vessel is entirely untouched by entropy, natural death, disease, and fatigue.
    

#### 2. Technique

- **Circle 1: The Autonomic Override** — Conscious management of basic involuntary reflexes, such as slowing your heart rate to stop heavy bleeding.
    
- **Circle 2: The Neural Tuning** — Micro-management of muscle recruitment and adrenaline, letting you tense to absorb impacts or flood your system with focus.
    
- **Circle 3: The Cellular Focus** — Targeted biological direction and localized healing, enabling you to consciously seal blood vessels or deaden nerves in an injured limb.
    
- **Circle 4: The Masterful Alignment** — Seamless multi-system synchronization of breathing, blood flow, and nervous output into a unified operational state without mental strain.
    
- **Circle 5: The Morphological Shift** — Deliberate structural and tissue alteration, allowing you to compress joints to squeeze through tight spaces or shift muscle mass to reinforce a struck limb.
    
- **Circle 6: The Transcendent Adaptation** — Rapid environmental and trauma-based evolution where your body dynamically mutates or hardens to neutralize recurring threats.
    
- **Circle 7: Absolute Biological Sovereignty** — Total mastery and rewrite of the physical vessel, giving you god-like dominion to completely reshape your anatomy or regrow lost limbs in seconds.
    

### Mage Archetype: The Reality Weaver

## 1. Form

- **Circle 1: The State Shifter** — Basic phase transitions, allowing you to melt ice into water, turn puddles into vapor, or crystallize moisture.
    
- **Circle 2: The Molecular Reorganizer** — Internal bonding and density manipulation, giving you the ability to soften a metal lock or harden dirt and stone.
    
- **Circle 3: The Material Transmuter** — Advanced compositional shaping, letting you actively reshape metals, stones, and organic matter into new geometric forms.
    
- **Circle 4: The Masterful Cohesion** — High-volume structural control, enabling you to disintegrate massive stone walls into dust or assemble scattered debris into a solid bridge.
    
- **Circle 5: The Exotic Composition** — Advanced material creation and atomic densification, compressing carbon into diamond-hard lattices or conjuring heavy-metal alloys.
    
- **Circle 6: The Transcendent Molecular** — Macro-scale physical transmutation, allowing you to directly turn incoming stone projectiles into dust or change water into acid.
    
- **Circle 7: Absolute Matter Dominion** — Ultimate mastery of physical substance, granting absolute command over the atomic structure to synthesize, erase, or rewrite environments.
    

## 2. Force

- **Circle 1: The Thermal Spark** — Basic thermodynamic manipulation to ignite flames, boil water, or draw heat out of a puddle to freeze it.
    
- **Circle 2: The Kinetic Push** — Directional momentum and force transfer, letting you project kinetic force through the air to push objects or deflect projectiles.
    
- **Circle 3: The Electromagnetic Flow** — Current and spectrum manipulation, enabling you to generate lightning arcs, bend light for invisibility, or short-circuit electronics.
    
- **Circle 4: The Masterful Equilibrium** — High-volume thermodynamic and kinetic control, such as flash-freezing an entire room or halting a charging vehicle's momentum.
    
- **Circle 5: The Supernatural Radiation** — Exotic particle and radiative force, projecting solar-grade heat, absolute zero cold fields, or laser-like beams of pure energy.
    
- **Circle 6: The Transcendent Thermodynamic** — Macro-scale energy rewriting, allowing you to spontaneously generate or completely erase energy within a designated zone.
    
- **Circle 7: Absolute Energy Dominion** — Ultimate mastery of thermodynamic and kinetic law, operating as a primary source and sink for all energy to channel cosmic-scale forces.
    

## Monk Archetype: The Ascetic Vessel

### 1. Flow

- **Circle 1: The Balanced Stride** — Absolute poise and center of gravity adjustments for total stability across narrow ledges or slick surfaces.
    
- **Circle 2: The Frictionless Flow** — Elimination of internal drag, allowing running, dodging, and striking to flow into one continuous motion without dead frames.
    
- **Circle 3: The Kinetic Redirect** — Alignment-based force management, using body geometry to smoothly route incoming momentum around or through you.
    
- **Circle 4: The Weightless Step** — Partial defiance of downward pull, letting you sprint across thin ice or drop from great heights without taking damage.
    
- **Circle 5: The Infinite Acceleration** — Compounding kinetic velocity where movement builds upon itself, turning attacks into an unstoppable vortex.
    
- **Circle 6: The Spatial Glissade** — Non-linear traversal across distance by treating physical space as a continuous ribbon to cross paths in a single fluid blur.
    
- **Circle 7: Absolute Inertial Mastery** — Total unity with gravity and motion, allowing you to walk up vertical walls, hover over open air, or dictate your own trajectory.
    

### 2. Mind

- **Circle 1: The Heightened Baseline** — Fine-tuned sensory reception that processes visual, auditory, and tactile input faster to catch subtle posture shifts or breathing patterns.
    
- **Circle 2: The Micro-Tell Reader** — Behavioral pattern matching of micro-expressions and muscle tension to anticipate and cleanly dodge incoming strikes.
    
- **Circle 3: The Environmental Harmony** — Seamless spatial integration where your awareness expands to read air currents and vibrations for navigating darkness.
    
- **Circle 4: The Predictive Calculation** — Multi-variable tactical forecasting where projectiles and attacks appear to move in slow motion for effortless deflection.
    
- **Circle 5: The Intent Reader** — Pre-cognitive thought perception, sensing the cognitive spark of intent before muscles contract to eliminate the effectiveness of feints.
    
- **Circle 6: The Omnidirectional Presence** — Spherical non-local awareness, registering reality equally in all 360 degrees simultaneously to render blindsiding impossible.
    
- **Circle 7: Absolute Real-Time Omniscience** — Perfect harmony with the flow of events, allowing your perception to move in lockstep with reality for serene mastery.
    

## Patron Archetype: The Conduit

### 1. Bond

- **Circle 1: The Shared Breath** — A momentary blending of presence during extreme crises, granting a flash of intuitive clarity mirroring the entity's stress management.
    
- **Circle 2: The Co-Pilot** — Alternating control of instinct where you invite the entity to manage specific physical processes to bypass human error.
    
- **Circle 3: The Partial Symbiosis** — Overlapping presence allowing you to sustain foreign mana and temporary biological changes like hardening skin or extra eyes.
    
- **Circle 4: The Dual Sovereignty** — Equal sharing of the vessel, merging your thoughts with the Patron's intent to double cognitive processing power and decision speed.
    
- **Circle 5: The Entity's Reach** — Voluntary vessel occupation where the entity takes primary control of your physical form to wield its full scale and power.
    
- **Circle 6: The Blurs of Identity** — Erasure of individual boundaries where you perceive yourself as the entity, natively carrying its cosmic weight in every action.
    
- **Circle 7: The Transcendent Pact** — Absolute unification and apotheosis, transforming your mortal form into a permanent physical anchor and localized cosmological force for the Patron.
    

### 2. Authority

- **Circle 1: The Ambient Aura** — A subtle psychological and environmental bleed where the Patron's emotional or environmental signature leaks into your surroundings.
    
- **Circle 2: The Imposed Presence** — Concentrated intimidation, letting you release a pulse of pressure that causes weak-willed opponents to feel physically weighed down.
    
- **Circle 3: The Borrowed Might** — Temporary structural enhancement via proxy, channeling a direct stream of the Patron's operational framework to augment your capacity.
    
- **Circle 4: The Domain Encroachment** — Localized environmental overwriting where physical space warps to resemble the Patron's home domain, disrupting elements within a radius.
    
- **Circle 5: The Avatar's Strike** — Manifestation of the entity's literal limb or weapon, allowing you to strike with spectral claws, tentacles, or crystalline edges.
    
- **Circle 6: The Absolute Mandate** — Imposition of a cosmic rule, projecting the Patron's authority to enforce absolute stillness or thematic restrictions over an area.
    
- **Circle 7: The Sovereign Extension** — Total dominion of the Patron's plane, projecting a massive bubble that completely rewrites local physics to make you an omnipotent ruler within your radius.
