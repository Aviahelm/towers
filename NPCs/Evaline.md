# Evaline — Supplier & Deal-Broker (Tower 7, Marketplace)

> Introduced in [Session 2 of "Ground Bee-f"](../sessions/ground_bee-f_2/Jade.md); also in [Lee.md](../sessions/ground_bee-f_2/Lee.md). **Canonical name: Evaline** (written "Evalin" in some Session 2 records — a spelling variant, now resolved). The party's supplier and the source of their **bracelet tech**.

## Canon (established)

- A **supplier** in the **Tower 7 Marketplace**.
- **The "Finch" deal:** the party dealt with her under the pseudonym **"Finch"**; **she did not know who they were**. Terms: **2 rations = 1 day of living** (eat, drink, sleep, hygiene, and "a couple of services").
- **Sealed the deal with a kiss** (Lee: "she sealed the deal with a kiss").
- **Memory-removal:** she **removed [Mallory's](../Characters/Mallory.md) memories** (Mallory no longer remembers why the party came — only who/when).
- The **party owes her one favour** (the deal was made on her behalf, via [Joe Dart](../Characters/Joe Dart.md) as "Finch").
