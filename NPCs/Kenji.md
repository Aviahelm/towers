# Kenji — Cobalt Order

> A **Cobalt Order mercenary** in Tower 7 and a tense, **potentially hostile** contact of [Yuntao](../Characters/Yuntao.md). Named in [Session 1 — "Judged"](../sessions/230926_judged_1/Lee.md); the message/reply is from [Session 2 — "Ground Bee-f"](../sessions/041026_ground_bee-f_2/Jade.md).

## Canon (established)

- A **mercenary** of the **Cobalt Order**, now in **Tower 7**.
- **Loyal to himself** — self-interested; not loyal to the Order and not to Yuntao.
- **Caught during a job, and Yuntao didn't care — let him be sent to Tower 7.** This is the root of Kenji's resentment toward Yuntao.
- **Potential hostility** toward [Yuntao](../Characters/Yuntao.md).
- **A fan of Han Yunna** — Yuntao's older sister (see [Yuntao](../Characters/Yuntao.md)); an awkward loyalty, given Yuntao was accused of her murder. **Han Yunna is confirmed dead** — killed by **Duke Evercrest** (a merchant); Yuntao killed Evercrest in revenge.
- **Session 3:** Yuntao told Kenji that Yunna is dead — **Kenji reacted with shock** (see [Lee's notes](../sessions/201026_small_problems_3/Lee.md)).
- **Crime:** **selling drugs** to others out of the **Cobalt Order's supply**.
- **Replied to [Yuntao's](../Characters/Yuntao.md) message** (the C5 Force research lead) — content of the reply not yet recorded.
