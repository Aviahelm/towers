A vendor/mechanic at the Tower 7 **Marketplace**.

## Canon (established)

- A **spliced human × octopus** — a creature part-human, part-octopus.
- A **mechanic / vendor** operating in the **Tower 7 Marketplace**.
- **Session 2:** the party **swindled him** — after waking from being choked out by [Mellathor](../Characters/Mellathor.md), [Yuntao](../Characters/Yuntao.md) **entered his shop still aggressive** (first visit) and **Plato spiked him**; on a **second visit**, Yuntao **successfully stole his C5 Force research notes**. He had been trying to **sell a robot for 20 rations** (Lee).
- **[Joe Dart](../Characters/Joe Dart.md) holds a distinct animosity toward him** — origin of the grudge unknown.
