# Thraxus — Chief of Sector 1 (Tower 7)

> Named in [Session 1 of "Judged"](../sessions/judged_1/Cam.md) and [Lee.md](../sessions/judged_1/Lee.md); documented in [Tower 7](../Towers/Tower_7.md). **Canonical spelling: Thraxus** (also written *Thraxis* in some records). The party met **one body** — the [Marketplace Owner](Marketplace%20Owner.md) (Session 2) — but the collective as a whole is still unmet.

## Canon (established)

- **Chief of Sector 1** (the industrial sector) of [Tower 7](../Towers/Tower_7.md).
- **Not a person — a collective robot with multiple bodies.** (Stated consistently across all session records.)
- Sector 1 = iron-ore industrialisation, automated; its stated danger is **"Dangerous Automation,"** with **room-sized drones** and the **Silent Time** nightly scan. Escape route is **technical**.
- **Previous S1 chief:** a very short man — a **gnome who revolutionised and automated the iron-ore industry** (records span ~20 cycles; 1 cycle = 1 year + 2 months).
- Reported on by [Jordan](Jordan.md), who knew both that Thraxus leads S1 and where the party could find a base.
- **Recruits people** (not just automation) to **mine ores** and to **patch where the machines can't work** — the **big drone** the party saw hovering/scouting in S1 is one such recruitment/asset. [Jordan](Jordan.md) has direct knowledge of this.
- **One body identified: the [Marketplace Owner](Marketplace%20Owner.md)** — the robot who runs the Tower 7 Marketplace (confirmed Session 3; the party met this instance in Session 2, before knowing it was Thraxus).
