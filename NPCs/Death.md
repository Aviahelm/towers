# Death — The Merchant (Tower 7)

> The merchant who haunts the Comms Tower area and trades with Sector 2. Resurrected [Mellathor](../Characters/Mellathor.md) in [Session 3 of "Match made in Hell — Small Problems"](../sessions/201026_small_problems_3/Lee.md); the table named him **Death**.

## Canon

- A **merchant** near the **Comms Tower**; frequents [Sector 2](../Towers/Tower_7.md) — can **sell things to Sector 2** or **take the party there (fast travel)**.
- **Sneaked up on [Joe Dart](../Characters/Joe Dart.md) despite Dart's heightened senses.**
- **Resurrected Mellathor** (killed by the elf's fire spells in the Comms Tower fight) with a snap of his fingers, in exchange for **a favour** — described at the table as a "fun" favour.
- Effect of his resurrection on Mellathor: came back **2 feet tall** with a **squeaky voice**.
- "We owe him now."
