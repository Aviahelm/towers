# Oritel — Mallory's Patron · **Tower 2**

> **Canon shift (10/6/2026):** Oritel is **not** an abstract or foreign patron — Oritel **is Tower 2**. A new, still-forming, **want-to-be god-like being** that is ascending into divinity. Everything Mallory's "Patron" path has implied now reads through this lens. The open questions below remain open, but they're now open in a much higher-stakes way.

## Canon (established)

- **Oritel is Tower 2** — a **new, want-to-be god-like entity**. Oritel is not one of the original Towers; Oritel is *becoming* one (or becoming something above the Tower concept entirely).
- **Power source: Mallory's souls.** Oritel **powers up off the souls Mallory gathers** — the "Patron" path is a **feeding channel**. Mallory collects souls (by any means, in any Tower) and Oritel consumes them to grow. Her "Patron Authority C1" is her standing as the one who feeds the Tower.
- **Manifestation: through Mallory.** Oritel is **brought to the party through [Mallory](../Characters/Mallory.md)** (Mallory is the character **portrayed by the player "Bella"**). Oritel rides into play **in/through Mallory's body** — Mallory is the **vessel / channel**, not the being. This is the "Patron Bond → identity unification" end of the Patron path made literal.
- **Silence during the arson (Session 1, canon).** When the district Mallory was tending burned down — a dozen or more children dead — Mallory tried to call Oritel and got **nothing**. Her guards found her in an intense prayer pose among the bodies, her body unknowingly wrapped in **bright blue magical flame** connecting to the dead. The silence is now more legible: did Oritel **withdraw**, **choose not to answer**, or **turn the flame toward the dead instead** (souls entering its economy)?
- Mallory's trial charge is **Arson and Voluntary Manslaughter** (not-guilty plea).

## Reframed Read on Prior Clues (canon, 10/6/2026)

- **The Gardener's "stop feeding the flames"** (Tower 6) reads as a **direct warning to Mallory** about what she's feeding Oritel with — the souls are fuel.
- **The Jeffreys are manufactured in Tower 2** (per [Tower 2](../Towers/Tower_2.md)). If Oritel *is* Tower 2, **Oritel is making the Jeffreys**.
- **Mallory's "bright blue flame"** at the arson may be **Oritel's signature** — a channel of the soul-harvest leaking through her as the feed.
- **Han Yunna's death** (killed by Duke Evercrest, the merchant) and the C5 Force research stolen in Session 2 may connect to the soul-harvest economy Oritel runs on.
