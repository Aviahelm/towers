# Oritel — Mallory's Patron

> Known only through [Mallory](../Characters/Mallory.md) and her trial record (last confirmed in [Session 1 of "Judged"](../sessions/judged_1/Cam.md)). This entry is thin — treat most of it as **open** until the table establishes more.

## Canon (established)

- **Mallory's Patron.** Mallory is a Patron-bonded character (see [Mallory](../Characters/Mallory.md)); she works with Oritel, travels, and helps communities.
- **Went silent during the arson.** When the district Mallory was tending burned down with a dozen or more children dead, Mallory tried to call Oritel for assistance — **"nothing. No response."** Her guards found her in an intense prayer pose among the bodies, her body unknowingly wrapped in bright blue magical flame connecting to the dead.
- Mallory's trial charge is **Arson and Voluntary Manslaughter** (not-guilty plea); what Oritel *is*, or why the link failed, has not been established.
