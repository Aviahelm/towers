# Jordan — Tiefling Guide (Tower 7, Sector 1)

> First met in [Session 1 of "Judged"](../sessions/judged_1/Cam.md); also in [Lee.md](../sessions/judged_1/Lee.md) and [Tower 7](../Towers/Tower_7.md). The party's current guide/servant and the provider of their base.

## Canon (established)

- **Tiefling**, a former prisoner in Tower 7, Sector 1 — had been in the **S1 prison for ~2 weeks**, i.e. the two weeks **before the party headed down to Tower 7**.
- **Survival-driven** — "wants to survive the best way he can." A **thief, with [Zim](Zim.md) and an unnamed third companion (a human, now dead):** the trio wanted a better life and **stole weapons from a noble in Tower 5** (the medieval civilisation; **placeholder: *Ser Aldric Vane***, unlikely to reappear). Caught, they were **offered death or descent to Tower 7 — and chose T7**, then **robbed other newcomers to survive**. This is what led to them being **imprisoned in Tower 7, Sector 1** before the party reached them.
- One of the prisoners the party met coming off the **Hardlight bridge**: Mellathor stomped one (a human — **Jordan's unnamed third companion from the trio above**) dead; Yuntao choked and seared another ([Zim](Zim.md)); Mallory pinned a third — **Jordan** — whom Dart then interrogated.
- **Gave the party their Sector 1 intelligence:** [Thraxus](Thraxus.md) leads S1, and where a usable base could be found.
- **Gave the party their base of operations** — his old 4-storey shoddy apartment on the outskirts of S1 near a cliff (front door trapped with shotgun + molotov, disarmed by Mallory; the side metal-sheet door is the real entrance). See [Tower 7](../Towers/Tower_7.md).
- **Warned the party about Silent Time** (the nightly scanning drone).
- Mallory treats him cordially (asked his name, left him rations); Dart keeps him as a "guide/pet." His **burnt friend [Zim](Zim.md)** (the prisoner Yuntao had maimed) was not killed — given rations + a molotov and left to survive elsewhere.

## Role

- Now the party's **guide / servant** in Tower 7; local knowledge of S1 (and, per his testimony, of [Thraxus](Thraxus.md)).
- **Alignment is a survival calculus:** a **free guide** when the party is useful or good to him; an **opponent** if cornered, exploited, or if defection serves him better. Reassess his stance each session based on how the party treats him.
- **Knows that [Thraxus](Thraxus.md) is recruiting people** (the big drone seen scouting in S1) to mine ores and patch where automation fails.
