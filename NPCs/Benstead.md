# Benstead — Blonde Cafeteria Gang Leader (Tower 7)

> The blonde with the finger mohawk and piercings; runs a gang out of the Sector 1 cafeteria. Encountered in [Session 3 of "Match made in Hell — Small Problems"](../sessions/201026_small_problems_3/Lee.md).

## Canon

- **Blonde**, with a **mohawk made of fingers** and piercings; the only blonde around.
- Sits in the cafeteria with "friends" (one with a crazed look); effectively **leads the gang** there.
- **Killed the weapons-shop kids' older sister.**
- Reminds him of **a woman** he once knew ("she was pretty too"); when asked about being afraid of a woman he points at the mohawk — "**not anymore**".
- Is **afraid of [Yuntao](../Characters/Yuntao.md)** and tries to leave the table when Mal and Mel join.
- Session 3: [Mellathor](../Characters/Mellathor.md) shoved another patron's tray at him; he **ate it and passed out**.
- [Mallory](../Characters/Mallory.md) and Yuntao have secretly resolved to **take night watch and kill Benstead** without telling the others.
