# Jeffrey — Tower Automaton (the "Jeffreys")

> Six identical tower automata, one each for **Towers 2–7** (Tower 1 has none), who operate in the **circle spire** and run the elevator between Towers 2–7. The thin judge-side enforcer in [Tower 6](../Towers/Tower_6.md) and [Tower 7](../Towers/Tower_7.md); also in [Lee.md](../sessions/judged_1/Lee.md) and [MEMORY.md](../MEMORY.md).

## Canon (established)

- **Jeffrey is a tower automaton, not a person** — a *class*, not one individual. **There are exactly 6: one each for Towers 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7.** **Tower 1 has none** (it doesn't need anyone).
- **All six are identical in build** — **manufactured in Tower 2** — **but have distinct personalities** (see "Known individuals" below).
- **They operate in the *circle spire*** — the structure that handles the **elevator connecting Towers 2–7**.
- **Duties:** aid the judges and **guard/operate the elevators to higher floors** (control movement *up*). The rule of thumb at the table: **"The Judge sentences; Jeffrey enforces."**

### Known individuals

- **Tower 7 Jeffrey** — **detached; doesn't care too much about people.** The Jeffrey who **defeated Tower 7 S2's previous chief** (a battle-hardened creature) when he tried to fight it.
- **Tower 6 Jeffrey** (a.k.a. "Jeffrey 1" — the "very thin man" in the judge's cell) — **a bit exhausted by the judge's demands, but loyal.** The instance that **ripped out Yuntao's eye** and **maimed Gee** as punishment (see [Lee.md](../sessions/judged_1/Lee.md)).
- **Towers 2–5 Jeffreys** — **exist (one each) but not yet encountered / not yet detailed.**
