# Zim — Jordan's Burnt Friend (Revenge Arc)

> The "burnt friend" of [Session 1 of "Judged"](../sessions/judged_1/Cam.md); friend of [Jordan](Jordan.md). **Named: Zim.** Set to return with a gang for revenge.

## Canon (established so far)

- **Former prisoner** in Tower 7, Sector 1 — **Jordan's friend**; the two were in the **S1 prison for ~2 weeks**, i.e. the two weeks **before the party headed down to Tower 7**.
- **Thief (with [Jordan](Jordan.md)):** the pair **stole weapons from a noble in Tower 5** (the medieval civilisation). This is why both of them ended up **imprisoned in Tower 7, Sector 1**.
- During the **Hardlight-bridge clash** (where the party first met [Jordan](Jordan.md)): [Mellathor](../Characters/Mellathor.md) charged and **kicked him, sending him crashing into [Yuntao](../Characters/Yuntao.md)** — leaving a **hoof print** on his face. **Yuntao** then **burned that hoof print deeper into his face**, choked him out, and **burned his throat / vocal cords**; left him unconscious and dragged by the collar.
- His friend — **Jordan's unnamed human companion (the trio's third member)** — was **stomped dead by Mellathor** in the same clash. [Jordan](Jordan.md) begged Mallory to kill Zim and end his misery; she refused.
- **Jordan begged Mallory to kill him** and end his misery; she refused — gave him **rations** and a **molotov** (as a flare / for self-defense).
- He **left to try to survive on the outskirts** of Sector 1.

## Revenge Arc (DM-planned — will pay off later)

- **Zim will return with a gang to hunt down the party** for revenge — for his **burned face** and **burned vocal cords**.
- The party is the target, but **Yuntao** is the one who inflicted the specific injuries — his grudge may zero in on him.
- The **molotov** Mallory gave him is a ready-made callback (and a weapon he still carries).
