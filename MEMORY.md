## World <a id="world"></a>

- The world is divided into **7 Towers**; each tower feels like its own world.
- Everyone can bend reality to a degree. **Circles act as "permissions"** — the higher your circles, the further you can manipulate the world and your abilities.
- **Circle gain rule (established in Judged S1):** you gain a circle by showing you fundamentally know how it works.

### The Towers (1 = highest)

| Tower | Theme |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Godhood |
| 2 | Cyberpunk / Steampunk |
| 3 | Our current world (magic unknown) |
| 4 | Megamall — the "comfort trap"; widest space; people stagnate here |
| 5 | Medieval town / first real civilisation (low fantasy) |
| 6 | Feral/Forest buffer zone — volcanoes, deserts, beasts, survival |
| 7 | Slums — poverty, technology as mere tools, least knowledge of the world |

### Classes & Paths (see CLASSES.md / SKILL TREE.md)

- **Knight** — Body (physicality/stamina → indestructibility) · Technique (physiology mastery → anatomical sovereignty)
- **Mage** — Form (matter/space → matter dominion) · Force (energy/axioms → reality's code)
- **Monk** — Flow (grace/movement → gravity freedom) · Mind (clarity/awareness → real-time omniscience)
- **Patron** — Bond (entity relationship → identity unification) · Authority (projection of patron power → total environmental rewriting)
