# Tower 6 — The Feral Buffer Zone

> World details known after "Judged", Session 1 (the party transited T6 in prison but never entered it).
> Tower 6 = the feral / forest **buffer zone** between the civilised towers and the slums below: volcanoes, deserts, vast forests, and monsters you have to fend off. A place riddled with danger where you learn to survive in the harshest environments.

## What Tower 6 Is Known For

- **Harsh, wild environment** — volcanoes, deserts, huge forests, beasts. Survival is the baseline condition, not a quest.
- **The prison system** — the prison judge sits in Tower 6. Prisoners descend through T6 in blindfolded boxes to be distributed to the lower towers.
- **Transit through it** — descending, **each floor adds a new weight on the inhibitors** and a fogginess settles on the mind. The party passed through T6 on the way to Tower 7.
- **Mellathor has gone between T7 and T6 before** — he knows a way across (route unknown; presumably pre-dating the prison).

## The Judge (Tower 6)

- The prison judge; **extremely fat**. Known mostly for cruelty and sentences, not mercy.
- Called Yuntao **"pretty boy"** repeatedly during the judgment.
- **Sentenced the party to Tower 7** — Yuntao specifically: the Judge *really wanted to kill him* but, "to be cruel", sent him to the bottom instead of a merciful death.
- **Gee the chef** — "got absolutely wasted" (hands, eyes, tongue removed), then **returned to Tower 5**.
- **The very thin man in the cell is Jeffrey** (one of "the Jeffreys"). The Judge sentences; Jeffrey enforces. ("Gee the chef?" was a guess from the cell — it's Jeffrey, not Gee.) The **Tower 6 Jeffrey** is **a bit exhausted by the judge's demands but loyal**.
- The punishments were carried out by the **Tower 6 Jeffrey** (it ripped out Yuntao's eye and maimed Gee). A **Jeffrey is a tower automaton, not a person** — **6 total (one per tower, Towers 2–7; Tower 1 has none), all identical in build but distinct in personality, built in Tower 2**. They operate in the **circle spire** (runs the elevator between Towers 2–7) and guard the elevators up. The Judge sentences; the Jeffrey enforces.

## Governance / People

- **Unknown.** No chief, council, or law of T6 has been established. (It's called the *buffer zone* — whether it governs itself at all is an open question.)

## Connections

- **To Tower 5:** Gee the chef returned here (well — to T5) after his sentencing; Yuntao's replacement eye technology is back in *his* tower (T5).
- **To Tower 7:** the party's entry into T7 came via the T6 prison; Mellathor has crossed between them before.
