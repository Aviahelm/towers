In this world you live in separate Towers, each level is distinct by a certain amount of knowledge and power. You live in one of those 7 Towers this world is separated by. Each Tower feels like their own world. Those are known to you as followed: 

  

Tower 7: The slums, they are crowded with masses of people, you live in poverty, you have absolutely nothing. People build up technology to do the easy tasks they can’t do themselves, they value technology little; it's just a tool to you. People know the least about the world around them here. We struggle together but nobody is your friend fully, everyone may seek power. 

  

Tower 6: The Feral/Forest also known as the Buffer zone, is a place full of danger, you have to learn how to survive the most difficult surroundings, Volcanos, deserts, huge forests, beasts to fend off and the wild that not everyone can survive but what choice do you have when you want to get further?

  

Tower 5: The Medieval town. The first space that seems like an actual Civilisation was created here and as the name says it, it's quite old school progressive.

  

Tower 4: The most boring tower to land on, people with ambitions rather pass by as quickly as possible before they find themselves stuck in comfort. People grew too anxious to lose their position, too comforted by peace to advance further. The perfect place for retirement. It's the widest space, the elevation up to Tower 3 too far to care enough to give up on its peace. Many people remain in their little squares, perhaps just a block and a half far for the entirety of their lives. Things are easier here and people just didn't feel like any advancements further are necessary. The question is, what does it take for you to go up further? How can you pass this space to get to Tower 3?
