# Tower 7 — The Slums

> World details learned during "Judged", Session 1 (prison arrival, Sector 1).
> Tower 7 = the lowest tower: slums, poverty, "absolutely nothing". People build technology only to do easy tasks they can't do themselves; they value technology little — it's just a tool. People here know the least about the world. You struggle together, but nobody is your friend fully, and everyone may seek power.

## Governance

- **Tower 7 governs itself.** There are **4 Chiefs of 4 sectors**, with rules and laws of their own.
- Current chief identities are **mostly unknown** (at S1's arrival — see below).
- **1 Cycle = 1 year + 2 months.** Previous chief records span ~20 cycles.

## The Four Sectors

| Sector | Character | Danger | Escape route |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 — Industrial** | Iron-ore industrialisation, automated. Slums as far as the eye can see from the entrance bridge. | **Automation** — "Dangerous Automation". Taken as slaves when found. Room-sized drones. | **Technical escape** |
| **S2 — Red Light District** | Formerly the battle zone. Now a red light district. | **Succubi**. | Become **crime bosses** |
| **S3 — Unknown** | Unknown — nobody will say. | Unknown. | **Figure out your own way** |
| **S4 — Battle Zone** | The new battle zone. "Only the strongest survive." | Fighting — constant. | **Fight your way out** (very difficult) |

## Rules & Mechanics (as experienced)

- **Silent Time** — a nightly giant drone (reported **4× Mellathor's size**) scans the buildings. Lights/activity restrictions apply.
- **Inhibitors** — implanted in everyone **at birth**. Weight and fogginess increase per floor descended while in prison transit (Tower 6 had the prison judge).
- **Sending Stones** — the inhibitors were upgraded to have sending-stone functionality: **20–25 words per use, costs stamina/fatigue.**
- **Rations** are the core scarcity resource — doled out by the authorities, traded, robbed.

## Escape

- The way out of Tower 7 (to Tower 6) is a **big metal door**.
- Sector-specific routes as listed in the table above (technical / crime / unknown / battle).
- Party's current objective (end of S1): **arm themselves with weapons/shields from the industrial zone** (Mallory's pitch, Yuntao + Mellathor agreed).

## Notable People & Groups (known in T7)

- **Jeffrey / "the Jeffreys"** — **tower automata, not people**: **6 total, one per tower (Towers 2–7; Tower 1 has none)**, all identical in build but distinct in personality, built in **Tower 2**. They operate in the **circle spire** (runs the elevator between Towers 2–7) and guard the elevators up. The **Tower 7 Jeffrey** is detached and **doesn't care much about people** — it defeated S2's previous chief when he tried to fight it.
- **The Judge (Tower 6)** — the prison judge; extremely fat; called Yuntao "pretty boy"; paired with a local **Jeffrey** (a Tower 2 automaton, the "very thin man" in the cell). Sentenced the party to Tower 7 (Gee the chef was maimed and sent back to Tower 5).
- **Cobalt Order members in T7:** [Junya](../NPCs/Junya.md) · [Kenji](../NPCs/Kenji.md) · [Parkin](../NPCs/Parkin.md) (red light district) — see the NPC files for details.
- **Marketplace Owner** — the robot who controls the Marketplace; impatient and strong, and shoppers defended him when he was attacked. **An instance (body) of [Thraxus](../NPCs/Thraxus.md)** — see [Marketplace Owner](../NPCs/Marketplace%20Owner.md).
- **Cobalt Order resentment (ideation — DM lens, not yet canon):** **high likelihood** that Tower 7 holds people who were **fucked over by the Cobalt Order** — a bedrock hostility toward any Cobalt Order presence (Yuntao, Kenji, Junya, Parkin). This pool overlaps with **Han Yunna's enemies**, and may reach the **Han parents' generation** → **generational beef** between Tower 7 and the Cobalt Order / Han family.
- **Mellathor's family:** Minotaurs last seen in **Sector 2**; supposedly moved elsewhere (per the judge's information).
- **Jordan** — tiefling, former prisoner; now the party's guide/servant; gave them their base.
