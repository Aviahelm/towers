# Tower 2 — Cyberpunk / Steampunk

> Per-tower detail file. See `Tower Notes.md` for the 7-tower overview (1 = highest) and `MEMORY.md` for campaign state.

## Theme
Cyberpunk / Steampunk. Tower 2 sits one step below the Godhood level of Tower 1.

## What's known
- **Home of the Jeffrey automata.** The **Jeffreys** — the tower's enforcer / guard automata — are **created here** in Tower 2.
  - **Not people** — they're **tower automata** (robot constructs). **There are 6: one each for Towers 2–7 (Tower 1 has none).** All six are **identical in build but have distinct personalities**; there is no single "Jeffrey."
  - **Purpose:** to **aid the judges** and to **guard the elevators that go to higher floors** (i.e., they control / gate movement *up*). They **operate in the *circle spire*** — the structure that runs the **elevator connecting Towers 2–7**.
  - Behavior in the field: brutal enforcers. The **Tower 6 Jeffrey** is exhausted by the judge but loyal — it maimed/blinded Yuntao (ripped out his eye). The **Tower 7 Jeffrey** is detached (doesn't care much about people) — it's the one the previous S2 chief died trying to fight.
- Fits the cyberpunk/steampunk theme: industrial-scale fabrication of autonomous constructs.

## Open Questions
