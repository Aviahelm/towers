## Background & Lore (canon)

- Travels with her Patron [Oritel](../NPCs/Oritel.md), helping communities.
- **The arson incident:** she received an urgent letter about a district hit by arson, rushed over, and found **dozens of drained, sunken child bodies** on a burning floor. She called for Oritel — **no response**. She was found in an **intense prayer pose**, surrounded by the dead, her body (unknowingly) engulfed in a **bright blue magical flame** connecting her to the other bodies.
- **Accused of:** Arson & Voluntary Manslaughter — **pleading not guilty**.

## The Oritel / Tower 2 Truth (canon, 10/6/2026)

- **Oritel is Tower 2** — a **new, want-to-be-god entity** ascending into divinity. See [Oritel](../NPCs/Oritel.md).
- **Mallory is the soul-farmhand.** The "Patron" bond is a **feeding channel**: Oritel **powers up off the souls Mallory gathers** (by any means, in any Tower). Her "Patron Authority C1" is her standing as the one who feeds the god.
- **Mallory is the vessel.** Oritel **manifests through Mallory's body** — the "Patron Bond → identity unification" end of the path made literal. When Oritel is active, it is *in* Mallory.
- **Reframe of prior clues:** The Gardener's (Tower 6) "stop feeding the flames" reads as a **direct warning to Mallory** about what she feeds Oritel with — the souls are fuel. Mallory's "bright blue flame" at the arson is plausibly **Oritel's signature** leaking through her as the channel.
- **Jeffreys:** the Tower 2 automata (see [Tower 2](../Towers/Tower_2.md)) may be Oritel's fabrication — the god making its own enforcers.
