## Appearance

- Lanky, agile build.
- Skin of rough, tooth-like **dermal denticles** (injury protection, drag reduction).
- **Cartilaginous** (not bony) frame — lighter and more flexible than bone.
- Near-360° vertical vision; eyes built for the dark.
- **Innate (hammerhead design):**
  - **Electrosensitivity** — the design's signature; detects electric fields as weak as a millionth of a volt (tracks prey, and later reality).
  - **Keen senses** — strong smell, hearing tuned to low / irregular frequencies, and a lateral-line "pressure map" of surroundings.
  - **Poison / venom resistance** (diet of stingrays, toadfish, porcupinefish, boxfish).
  - **Agile & quick** — sharp turns, ~20mph bursts; hunts at dawn/dusk.
  - **Social / pack** — hunts in groups but can go solo; opportunistic apex predator.
  - **Apex-hunter mentality** — prey regulation, "killing parasites to society" → the assassin / contract-killer role.

## Personality & Motivation

- **Emotionally intelligent sociopath** — reads people perfectly, feels for them not at all.
- **Does the job to get paid.** Won't kill unless he has to, or is employed to.
- **Gentle by default** — "seems whatever you want him to be."
- Prefers **subtlety, stealth, and covert / indirect kills** over loud brawls: strong knowledge of poisons, hidden weapons, and ranged weapons.
- Also a **patient tank-style brawler** when it comes to it — takes hits, waits for the perfect counter.
- Excellent **scout / utility**: enhanced senses make him the recon and trap-disarm specialist.

## Background & Lore (canon)

- Born into a **merfolk-mercenary group in Tower 5**; the **smallest of his litter**, so he had to prove his worth.
- That drive led him to develop his **electrosensitivity enhancement** and **defensive hardening** as a child.
