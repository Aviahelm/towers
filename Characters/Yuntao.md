## Personality & Motivation

- **Core traits** — Defensive, Arrogant, Self-Assured.
- **Fears** — losing everything he's worked for; not being the strongest / not being capable.
- **Ideals** — (1) You must be the strongest; survival means squashing competition. (2) Be the first to strike, but know your next move before you do. (3) Authority is optional — prove you're worth listening to.
- **Flaws** — cocky behind the Azure Dragon Heir reputation; polite and put-together, but 0-to-100 fast. When his worldview is shaken, his impatience and short fuse flare. Will use dirty tricks if they win.
- **Motivation** — build his way back up to **Tower 5** and claim the **leadership of the Cobalt Order**.

## Background & Lore (canon)

- Of the **Han family** and the **Cobalt Order** — a well-connected Tower 5 **clan** with influence over arms dealing (~~criminal organisation~~ → reclassified as a clan).
- The current generation had **two heirs, the "Azure Dragon Heirs":** Yuntao and his older sister **Han Yunna**. Both were raised to lead, but only one could claim the title — a **leadership duel** was set.
- Yuntao built a reputation as powerful but a "loose cannon"; Yunna was the more diplomatic, popular choice and was rumoured to hold the upper hand in training.
- **A week before the duel, Yunna vanished**, last seen entering **Duke Evercrest's** manor. Yuntao stormed the manor to investigate (not wanting the duel forfeited by absence) and was found with the **bodies of Duke Evercrest and Yunna**, covered in blood, having **killed a guard** who tried to detain him.
- **Charged** with the double homicide. The family — who could have cleared a simple murder — was **forced to give Yuntao over** for diplomacy and to preserve the Order.
- **Confirmed: Han Yunna is dead** — she was killed by **Duke Evercrest**, the **merchant** Yuntao killed. The manor night was Yuntao's **revenge**: he killed Evercrest, so the double-homicide charge was one murder committed and one found. What the Han family and the Cobalt Order knew of this is not yet recorded.
